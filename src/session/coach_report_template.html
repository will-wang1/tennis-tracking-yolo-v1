        Players

        PlayerOn cameraDistanceMovingWaiting off courtIdleHits (at least)

        Each player is followed across the whole session, joined up by clothing colour. Distance is measured near the camera and estimated at the far end; "far part estimated" shows how much of a total is the rougher kind. "Waiting off court" is time standing still for 3 seconds or more behind a baseline or beside the court while a drill is running - waiting for a turn. "Idle" is all such still time, on court too (ready position counts). Hits count only racket contacts the system is sure of, so each is a minimum.

        IdentityOn cameraDistanceAvg speedMovingHitsWhereTrust

        An identity is one continuous stretch of one person on camera, not a named player. Someone who is hidden for a while, or a camera cut, starts a new identity. "Moving" is the share of their time spent covering ground at 0.5 m/s or more. "Hits" counts only racket contacts the system is sure of, so each is a minimum.
