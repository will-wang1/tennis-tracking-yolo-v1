        Players

        PlayerOn cameraDistanceMovingHitsStroke mix (hits)Hand

        Each player is followed across the whole session, joined up by clothing colour. Distance is measured near the camera and estimated at the far end; "far part estimated" shows how much of a total is the rougher kind. Hits and stroke mix count only swings with the ball within reach.

        Tracking detail: every continuous stretch of tracking
        IdentityOn cameraDistanceAvg speedMovingHitsWhereTrust

        An identity is one continuous stretch of one person on camera, not a named player. Someone who is hidden for a while, or a camera cut, starts a new identity. "Moving" is the share of their time spent covering ground at 0.5 m/s or more. "Hits" counts only racket contacts the system is sure of, so each is a minimum.
